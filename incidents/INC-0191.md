## What happened (say this in an interview)

The payload entered SCIENCE_MODE. Current went to **~2.7 A** instead of the usual **~0.9 A**. Heater B was off. Bus voltage stayed above the 26.5 V warn — this family is caught on payload current, not the bus. Root cause: payload converter latched high. Same fault family as the PAY-002 contrast tape.

## What was ruled out

- **Heater B** — never enabled; current stayed ~0 A. **[OBSERVED]**
- **Bus undervoltage as the story** — `EPS.bus_voltage` did not cross 26.5 V. The entry was payload current. **[OBSERVED]**
- **Healthy SCIENCE_MODE** — 0.9 A cannot explain 2.7 A. **[DERIVED]**

## Root cause (close-out)

Payload power converter latched into overcurrent on SCIENCE_MODE entry: ~3× the healthy science baseline. **[DOCUMENTED]** at close-out; this was a **[HYPOTHESIS]** until current was compared to the last healthy science pass.

Matches fault library `PAYLOAD_POWER_SPIKE` (FAULT-002).

## Why a later payload warn should retrieve this

If an investigation of a SCIENCE_MODE window surfaces INC-0191, the operator can see that **payload 3×, heater idle, no bus-voltage warn** is already a closed family. Do not walk EPS-17 and inhibit Heater B.
