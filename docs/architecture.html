Architecture · Aurora-1

Sealed tape · AI in cobalt

The machine stops at a recommendation.

ORBIT does not detect the alarm and does not uplink a command. Opening a case seals a time window from the archive into durable evidence. The console assembles tape, tools, procedure, and a similar prior so a human can decide why — then files the close.
