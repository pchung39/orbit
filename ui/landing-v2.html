02 · The test that shaped the product

Same alarm. Same confounder.
Different answer.

Both cases drop the bus below warn just after science mode starts. The heater line is what tells them apart: one crosses the procedure's 2× bar, the other doesn't. A good investigator has to know when to act and when to hold.

I built the hold case first. Turning the heater off would lift the bus in its replay too, because shedding a 2 A load always helps, but that doesn't make the heater the cause. ORBIT holds, moves the case to Watching, and offers heater-off as EPS-17's isolation test, which needs operator authorization.

04 · The whole life of a case

A recommendation is the middle.
Not the end.

01

Open

Seal the telemetry window from the alarm.

02

Investigate

Weigh heater, payload, and battery against the procedure.

03

Decide

Compare three replayed futures. The operator chooses.

04

Act

Approved, recorded, and replayed. Never uplinked by ORBIT.

05

Verify

Check recovery against the procedure's last step.

06

Close

File the close-out with the decision and its outcome.

Evidence too weak? → Hold, and the case stays open as Watching.
